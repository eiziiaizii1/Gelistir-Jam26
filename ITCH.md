# ICE AS HELL

### SLIDE. JUMP. SURVIVE THE INFERNO.

You are a block of ice, loose on a mountain in Hell.

There is no brake. There is no health bar. There is only **how much of you is left** — and the
whole mountain is trying to melt it. Lava rivers, falling meteors, molten pillars, and a slope
that just keeps getting faster.

Get to the bottom before you're a puddle.

---

## 🧊 How it plays

You never slow down. The only thing you control is *where* you go — and you steer by getting
**slapped** by a giant hand.

- **Melting is your clock.** The smaller you get, the lighter you get — and light ice flies
  further with every slap. Being half-melted is dangerous *and* fun.
- **Hazards take a bite.** Lava rivers, lava columns, meteor rain, pillars, tentacles. Touch
  them and watch the ice bar drop.
- **Speed pays.** Neon boost pads and launch ramps fling you down the mountain — the ramps are
  real physics, so a good approach means real air.
- **Escape.** Reach the end of the run and you're out. Melt first and Hell keeps you.

---

## 🖱️ Controls

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold Left Mouse + swipe left / right** | Slap yourself sideways — this is your steering |
| **Hold Left Mouse + push mouse forward** | Dash |
| **Right Mouse** | Jump *(let go early for a shorter hop)* |

Swipe **harder** for a stronger slap. One swipe = one slap, so release and swing again — this is
a game of committed, deliberate hits, not held-down steering.

> **Tip:** don't fight the slope. Set up your line early, keep your speed, and use the ramps to
> fly straight over the lava instead of swerving around it.

## ⚡ Features

- 🔥 A full-speed downhill slide through Hell, powered by real physics — no rails, no fake speed
- 🖐️ Slap-based steering you won't find anywhere else
- 🌋 Lava rivers, meteor rain, and a mountain of things that want you melted
- 🚀 Boost pads and launch ramps for those "did that just work?" moments
- 📈 An endless procedural slope — the run is never quite the same twice
- 🎥 A camera that goes wide and shaky the faster you go
- 🏆 Arcade trick scoring for air time and near misses
